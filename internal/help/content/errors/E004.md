# E004 — record affects the wrong domain

**Cause.** Decision records are domain-pure. A **PDR** (product decision) may
only `affect` targets under `product.*`; an **ADR** (architecture decision) may
only `affect` targets under `engineering.*`. This error means a decision
record's `affects` list reaches into the other domain.

**Why.** Reasoning decomposes by domain: what looks like one cross-domain
decision is almost always a product decision (the *what*) plus an engineering
decision (the *how*). Keeping records domain-pure keeps that split honest.

**Fix — pick the shape that matches your intent:**

- The cross-domain link is a *dependency between elements*: drop the
  out-of-domain target from `affects` and declare a `fulfills` / `dependsOn`
  edge between the elements instead.
- It's really a *modification event* touching both domains: record it as a
  **change record** (CR), which is the one record type allowed to cross domains.
- The record is genuinely single-domain: remove the stray target.

See `intent help judgment:which-domain-owns-this-decision`.
